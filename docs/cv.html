Research Experience

Master’s Thesis Intern — Statistics Netherlands (CBS), 2026

  • Processed linked administrative register data across five institutional domains (work, education, household, family, and neighbourhood) within the CBS microdata environment, covering approximately 17 million Dutch residents.
  • Developed an adjusted multiplexity measure to account for administrative overlap between family and household registers, and linked the population-scale multilayer network to survey data (\(N\) = 28,483) to investigate how the organisation of social ties across domains relates to generalised trust.
  • Built a reproducible R pipeline using data.table, arrow, and DuckDB for large-scale network computation.
  • Collaborating on a CBS discussion paper based on the thesis, with a research presentation scheduled for the ODISSEI Conference in November 2026.

Statistical Consulting Project — Leiden University, 2025-2026

  • Developed a size-adjusted network density measure of classroom cohesion from peer-nomination data.
